# Example 6 — 面试一个 BIOS 工程师候选人

**Before**（原文，未修改）：

> Yesterday, my boss asked me to join the interview for a BIOS engineer cadidate. I didn't get the resume before, so when I come to the office, I spent a few minutes to quick go through the resume. in general, It looked good to me, she graduated from 南京航空航天大学, cet6 had worked in BYO the BIOS vendor for 3 years. I thought the experiences and background are good enough for the current role. but when boss asked her a few questions about her previous tasks, she could talk nothing. I saw her has 2 years gap which is quite similiar with me, I could 感同身受，I'd like to give her more chance and asked a few easy questions,just want her to show more, but she seems still didn't get it. so eventually, both Boss and HR thought she may not fit the job. It's a pity, I think maybe she didn't prepare well for it and lost the chance.

**After**（日常口语版）：

> Yesterday my boss had me sit in on an interview for a BIOS engineer candidate. I hadn't seen her resume beforehand, so when I got to the office I spent a few minutes skimming it. Overall it looked fine — she graduated from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, passed CET-6, and did three years at BYO, a BIOS vendor. I figured her background was good enough for the role. But when my boss asked her about her past work, she couldn't say a thing. I noticed she's got a two-year gap, just like I did, so I felt for her. I wanted to cut her some slack and asked a few easy questions, just to get her to open up — but she still didn't get it. In the end, both my boss and HR thought she probably wasn't a good fit. It's a shame. I think she just didn't prepare well and blew the chance.

**Key fixes**：

| 生硬 | 地道 |
|------|------|
| to quick go through | to quickly go through / skim |
| could talk nothing | couldn't say anything / drew a blank |
| her has 2 years gap | she has a two-year gap |
| similiar with me | similar to mine |
| 感同身受 | empathize / feel for her |
| give her more chance | cut her some slack / throw her a lifeline |
| may not fit the job | might not be a good fit |

**可迁移表达**：`feel for someone` · `draw a blank` · `cut someone some slack` · `a two-year gap` · `not a good fit`
