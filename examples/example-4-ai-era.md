# Example 4 — AI 时代最重要的能力

**Before**（原文，未修改）：

> I suffered laied off 2 years ago, after gapped 1.5 years, now I'm back to work. Although I've got a job but this experience drived me to think about what is my strength, how can I live in the future if got laid off again. I believe everyone should admit AI is very powerful, It's even much better than human being in many areas. to be honest, I'm quite anxious and worried about. I often think about what is the most important capablity in AI times ? last night, I happed to see a small video by zhang zara. she's talking with someone about this topic, the conclusion is we need to be high agency or be a good 出题人. after that 小视频 recommand another video, It says we should act as 苏格拉底 while using AI agent. in fact, I also has the same viewpoints, the most import thing maybe to ask the right questions, It's far more import than any other skills. what do you think ?

**After**（日常口语版）：

> I got laid off two years ago, and after a year and a half off, I'm back to work. I've got a job now, but the whole thing got me thinking: what are my strengths, and how would I get by if it happened again? I think we all have to admit AI is insanely powerful — in a lot of areas it's way better than us. Honestly, I'm pretty anxious about it. I keep wondering what the most important skill will be in the AI era. Last night I happened to see a short video by Zhang Zara, where she was chatting with someone about exactly this. Their takeaway: we need to be "high agency," or a good question-asker. Then the video recommended another one saying we should be like Socrates when using AI. And honestly, I agree — the key thing is asking the right questions, way more than any other skill. What do you think?

**Key fixes**：

| 生硬 | 地道 |
|------|------|
| suffered laied off | got laid off |
| after gapped 1.5 years | after a year and a half off |
| Although... but... | 只保留一个（中文"虽然…但是"陷阱）|
| drived me | drove me / got me thinking |
| better than human being | better than humans |
| 出题人 | question-setter / the one who frames the problem |
| high agency | high agency（✅ 已用对）|

**可迁移表达**：`get laid off` · `high agency` · `question-setter` · `make me reflect on` · `in the age of AI`
