# Example 7 — 自我反思：好为人师与情绪化抗拒

**Before**（原文，未修改）：

> I talked to many people today,sorry not only people but also AI. (maybe not that much, just comparing with the usual). I got some insights from these discussion. One thing is that I realize I'd like to give statement and tends to prove it, "好为人师" this is a bad habit/behavior, I have to get rid of it. everyone has his viewpoint, It's based on everyone's experience, It's very personal and probably wrong because too few samples prove nothing. another thing is I become very emotional when talk with people when someone ask me to do sth, even It's right, but I tends to reject it at the first place. this is also a bad charactoers. how can I fix these bad characters? Maybe I need to writ it down,review and selfcriticize it everyday.

**After**（日常口语版）：

> I talked to a lot of people today — actually not just people, AI too. And I got a couple of takeaways from those chats. One is, I realized I like to lecture — I make statements and try to prove them. That's a bad habit I've gotta shake. I mean, everyone has their own take, based on their own experience, and it's super personal and probably wrong — a few samples don't prove anything. The other thing is, I get emotional in conversations: when someone asks me to do something, even if it's reasonable, my knee-jerk reaction is to say no. That's another flaw. How do I fix these? Maybe I should write them down and review them every day.

**Key fixes**：

| 生硬 | 地道 |
|------|------|
| sorry not only people | actually not just people |
| give statement | make statements |
| 好为人师 | love to lecture / be preachy / a know-it-all |
| I tends to | I tend to |
| reject it at the first place | my knee-jerk reaction is to say no |
| too few samples prove nothing | a handful of examples proves nothing |
| selfcriticize | reflect on / review |

**可迁移表达**：`love to lecture` · `make statements` · `knee-jerk reaction` · `a handful of examples proves nothing` · `reflect on`
