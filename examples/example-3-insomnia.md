# Example 3 — 失眠

**Before**（原文，未修改）：

> I slept late last night, I don't know why. maybe I drank too much tea during daytime or a classmate refered a new opptunity from big internet comany which impacted my emotion. I thought I can keep peace now since I'm getting old, It turned out I'm not as strong as I thougt. It seems I could be easilly impacted by outside tiny things. 失眠 is painful, I kept 辗转反侧 but still can't sleep, after I got up at 2 am to have a pee, Thank God, eventually I got asleep.

**After**（日常口语版）：

> I **couldn't sleep** last night, and I **have no idea why**. Maybe I had too much tea **during the day**, or a friend telling me about a new gig at a big tech company **got me all worked up**. I thought I'd be **pretty chill** by now, but **turns out I'm not as tough as I thought**. I guess I still **get thrown off by little things**. Man, **insomnia is rough** — I **tossed and turned** for hours. Then I got up at 2 **to pee**, and thank God, I finally **crashed**.

**Key fixes**：

| 生硬 | 地道 |
|------|------|
| I slept late（⚠️ 意思是"睡懒觉"） | couldn't sleep / couldn't fall asleep |
| keep peace | keep my cool / stay calm |
| impact my emotion | get me worked up / affect my mood |
| 辗转反侧 | toss and turn |
| got asleep | fell asleep / drifted off / crashed |

**可迁移表达**：`couldn't sleep` · `keep my cool` · `get worked up` · `toss and turn` · `fall asleep` · `get thrown off by little things`
