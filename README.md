# idiomatic-english-coach

> Turn "correct but stiff" English into **natural, idiomatic** English.
> 把「正确但生硬」的英文，打磨成「地道自然」的英文。

一套 AI 口语教练工作流，帮你从"语法正确但生硬"进阶到"地道、自然、多样"，并附一套记忆系统，让学到的东西真正记得住、说得出口。

An AI coaching workflow that takes you from *grammatically correct but awkward* to *natural, idiomatic and varied* — with a retention system so what you learn actually sticks.

---

## The problem · 痛点

**中文：** 你能无障碍对话、听力也没问题、语法基本都对，但说出来的英文还是像"翻译腔"——**正确，却生硬**。你不满足于"能交流"，你想"说得好"。

**English:** You can hold a conversation, you understand what people say, and your grammar is mostly fine. But your English still sounds like translation — **correct, yet stiff**. You don't just want to get by; you want to sound good.

---

## The core idea · 核心理念

**每天写一段「今天发生的小事」。**

**中文：**
- 生活里、工作中真实发生的一段小事——不用专门找话题，随手记录就行。
- 贴近真实场景，不是背课文，是你真经历过的事。
- **有趣、零负担、容易坚持**——这是能天天做下去的关键。

> 先写下来，再由 AI 帮你打磨、改写、提炼，最后开口说出来。

**English:**
- One short paragraph about something that *actually happened* today — at work or in life. No need to hunt for a topic.
- It's real and close to your own situation, not textbook English.
- Because it's interesting and zero-pressure, **it's easy to stick with every day**.

> Write first, get it polished, then say it out loud.

---

## How it works · 怎么用

**中文（3 步）：**

1. 把 [`coach-prompt.md`](coach-prompt.md) 的内容粘给任意 AI（Claude / ChatGPT 等）。
2. 用英文写一段今天发生的小事。**别先润色**——越"中式"、越原始，教练越能对症下药。
3. 看改写，大声读，然后把 Anki 卡导入，靠间隔重复记住。

**English (3 steps):**

1. Paste [`coach-prompt.md`](coach-prompt.md) into any AI (Claude, ChatGPT, …).
2. Write one paragraph in English about something that happened today. **Don't polish it first** — the rawer and more "Chinglish", the better the coaching.
3. Read the rewrites aloud, then import the Anki cards and let spaced repetition do the rest.

---

## The core loop · 核心闭环

```
写 write → 分析改写 analyze & rewrite → 朗读 read aloud → 复述 retell (Anki 间隔)
```

- **写**：建表达库（writing builds your expression library）
- **朗读**：练嘴，肌肉记忆（reading aloud trains your mouth）
- **复述**：练脑，把"认得"变成"说得出"（retelling at spaced intervals turns recognition into production）

---

## What you'll get · 能达到的效果

**中文：**

- 一句话被改写成三个版本（正式 / 日常 / 讲故事），表达**更多样、更生动**
- 每个话题学到 3~7 个**地道表达**，纠正中式直译和词义陷阱（比如 `slept late` ≠ 失眠）
- 通过 Anki 间隔重复 + 复述，把"我认得"升级成"我说得出口"
- 坚持 30 天，明显感到说话更顺、更自然、更有底气

**English:**

- Every sentence gets rewritten three ways (formal / casual / storytelling), so your English becomes more varied and vivid
- Each topic teaches 3–7 idiomatic expressions and fixes Chinglish habits and meaning traps (e.g. `slept late` doesn't mean insomnia)
- With Anki spaced repetition + retelling, "I recognize it" becomes "I can say it"
- Stick with it for 30 days and you'll noticeably sound smoother and more natural
